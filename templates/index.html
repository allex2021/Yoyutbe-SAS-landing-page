❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: How does the Auto-Bleep & Censor bar work?

A: The system scans the audio stream with Whisper for profanity, then mixes a silent filter during those intervals and overlays a black censor bar on the center-lower portion of the video.

Q: What is Beat-Sync and how do I use it?

A: Beat-Sync analyzes the transient rhythm peaks in the music file. It then snaps the start times of the caption words to the closest beat drop, making the captions feel alive and synced to the music.

Q: Does voice translation dubbing support multiple languages?

A: Yes! It translates the text transcript to your target language, generates the voiceover audio, speeds it up or down to align with the video time, and swaps the original track with the translated voice.

Q: How is the Virality Score calculated?

A: We parse the text transcript and metadata to Gemini. It evaluates the hook efficiency, retention quality, and supplies actionable suggestions.
